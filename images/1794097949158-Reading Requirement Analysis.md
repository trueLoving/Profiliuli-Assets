---

## Target Competencies

To consistently achieve Band 8, learners should be able to:

### Reading Ability

- Read long academic passages efficiently.
- Understand the overall structure of an article.
- Locate specific information quickly.
- Distinguish between main ideas and supporting details.
- Understand complex academic arguments.
- Read without translating word-by-word.
- Maintain reading speed under time pressure.

---

### Language Ability

- Understand a wide range of academic vocabulary.
- Recognize synonyms and paraphrases immediately.
- Understand complex sentence structures.
- Follow logical relationships between ideas.
- Interpret reference words accurately.
- Understand collocations and natural expressions.

---

### Exam Ability

- Skim passages to identify the overall structure.
- Scan efficiently for specific information.
- Match questions with relevant paragraphs.
- Handle different question types confidently.
- Allocate time effectively across all three passages.
- Maintain accuracy under exam conditions.

---

## Success Criteria

The learner should be able to:

- Score **35–36 correct answers** consistently.
- Finish all three passages within 60 minutes.
- Locate answers quickly without rereading excessively.
- Recognize most paraphrases naturally.
- Understand complex passages without translating sentence by sentence.

# 4. Question Type Analysis

Each question type has its own solving strategy and language requirements.

---

## Matching Headings

### Purpose

Identify the main idea of each paragraph.

### Solving Strategy

1. Read the headings.
2. Identify keywords.
3. Read the first and last sentences of each paragraph.
4. Match the central idea instead of details.

### Required Language Knowledge

- Vocabulary
- Paraphrasing
- Logical understanding

### Common Mistakes

- Focusing on details instead of the main idea.
- Matching similar headings too quickly.

---

## True / False / Not Given

### Purpose

Determine whether the statement agrees with the passage.

### Solving Strategy

1. Locate the relevant paragraph.
2. Compare meanings carefully.
3. Distinguish between contradiction and missing information.

### Required Language Knowledge

- Vocabulary
- Grammar
- Logical comparison
- Paraphrasing

### Common Mistakes

- Using personal knowledge.
- Confusing False with Not Given.

---

## Yes / No / Not Given

### Purpose

Identify the author's opinion.

### Solving Strategy

- Identify opinion statements.
- Distinguish facts from opinions.
- Compare the author's viewpoint with the question.

### Required Language Knowledge

- Vocabulary
- Opinion expressions
- Logical reasoning

---

## Summary Completion

### Solving Strategy

- Read the summary first.
- Predict the missing word type.
- Locate the relevant section.
- Look for paraphrases.

### Required Language Knowledge

- Vocabulary
- Synonyms
- Paraphrasing
- Grammar

---

## Sentence Completion

### Solving Strategy

- Predict grammar and word class.
- Locate the relevant sentence.
- Verify grammar after completion.

### Required Language Knowledge

- Vocabulary
- Grammar
- Paraphrasing

---

## Matching Information

### Solving Strategy

- Read the questions first.
- Scan paragraphs quickly.
- Match ideas rather than keywords.

### Required Language Knowledge

- Vocabulary
- Paraphrasing
- Logical understanding

---

## Matching Sentence Endings

### Solving Strategy

- Read sentence stems.
- Predict logical completion.
- Match meaning rather than wording.

### Required Language Knowledge

- Vocabulary
- Grammar
- Logic

---

## Matching Names

### Solving Strategy

- Identify people mentioned.
- Track each person's ideas.
- Match opinions accurately.

### Required Language Knowledge

- Vocabulary
- Opinion expressions

---

## Multiple Choice

### Solving Strategy

- Read all options.
- Compare differences.
- Eliminate distractors.

### Required Language Knowledge

- Vocabulary
- Paraphrasing
- Reading comprehension

---

## Table Completion

### Solving Strategy

- Understand the table structure.
- Predict answer type.
- Locate corresponding information.

### Required Language Knowledge

- Vocabulary
- Grammar

---

## Diagram Labelling

### Solving Strategy

- Understand the diagram.
- Predict labels.
- Locate the relevant description.

### Required Language Knowledge

- Technical vocabulary
- Spatial expressions

---

## Short Answer Questions

### Solving Strategy

- Read the questions.
- Scan efficiently.
- Follow the word limit.

### Required Language Knowledge

- Vocabulary
- Detail comprehension

---
