# 1. Target

## Target Score

| Item | Target |
|------|--------|
| Module | Speaking |
| Target Band | **7.5–8.0** |

---

## Target Competencies

To achieve Band 7.5–8.0, learners should be able to:

### Speaking Ability

- Respond naturally without memorized answers.
- Speak fluently with minimal hesitation.
- Develop ideas logically and coherently.
- Express abstract opinions confidently.
- Support opinions with explanations and examples.
- Adapt language to different topics.

---

### Language Ability

- Use a wide range of vocabulary accurately.
- Use natural collocations and idiomatic expressions.
- Produce varied grammatical structures.
- Pronounce words clearly with natural stress and intonation.
- Retrieve language automatically without translation.

---

### Exam Ability

- Understand each part's objective.
- Expand answers appropriately.
- Maintain fluency under pressure.
- Organize ideas quickly.
- Recover smoothly from mistakes.

---

## Success Criteria

The learner should be able to:

- Speak continuously with few unnatural pauses.
- Explain opinions clearly.
- Use varied vocabulary and sentence structures.
- Pronounce naturally and intelligibly.
- Perform confidently across all three parts.

# 2. Test Overview

## Test Structure

| Part | Time | Purpose |
|------|------|----------|
| Part 1 | 4–5 min | Personal questions |
| Part 2 | 1 min preparation + 2 min speaking | Individual long turn |
| Part 3 | 4–5 min | Abstract discussion |

---

## Part Characteristics

### Part 1

Topics include:

- Work
- Study
- Hometown
- Family
- Hobbies
- Daily life

Focus:

- Natural conversation
- Everyday vocabulary
- Fluency

---

### Part 2

Task:

Receive a cue card.

Preparation:

- 1 minute

Speaking:

- Around 2 minutes

Focus:

- Sustained speech
- Story organization
- Detail development

---

### Part 3

Discussion:

- Abstract topics
- Social issues
- Comparison
- Cause & Effect
- Advantages & Disadvantages
- Future Trends

Focus:

- Critical thinking
- Logical reasoning
- Complex language

---

# 4. Part Analysis

---

## Part 1

### Purpose

Assess everyday communication.

### Strategy

- Answer directly.
- Extend naturally.
- Give one reason or example.

### Required Language Knowledge

- Everyday vocabulary
- Daily-life expressions
- Simple sentence patterns

### Common Mistakes

- Answers too short.
- Memorized responses.
- Repetition.

---

## Part 2

### Purpose

Assess sustained speaking ability.

### Strategy

- Plan quickly.
- Follow a simple structure.
- Cover every cue point.
- Continue speaking until time ends.

### Required Language Knowledge

- Storytelling language
- Time expressions
- Linking devices
- Descriptive vocabulary

### Common Mistakes

- Finish too early.
- Ignore cue points.
- Lose logical flow.

---

## Part 3

### Purpose

Assess higher-level communication.

### Strategy

- Explain ideas.
- Compare perspectives.
- Analyze causes and effects.
- Give balanced opinions.

### Required Language Knowledge

- Academic vocabulary
- Abstract concepts
- Logical connectors
- Complex sentence structures

### Common Mistakes

- Stay at a personal-example level.
- Lack of analysis.
- Weak idea development.
