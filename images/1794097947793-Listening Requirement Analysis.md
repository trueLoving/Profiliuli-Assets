# 1. Target

## Target Score

| Item | Target |
|------|--------|
| Module | Listening |
| Target Band | **8.0** |
| Correct Answers | **35–36 / 40** |

---

## Target Competencies

To consistently achieve Band 8, learners should be able to:

### Listening Ability

- Understand everyday conversations with ease.
- Follow academic discussions accurately.
- Understand long monologues and lectures.
- Process information without translating word-by-word.
- Keep concentration throughout the entire test.

---

### Language Ability

- Understand a wide range of vocabulary.
- Recognize synonyms instantly.
- Understand paraphrased expressions.
- Recognize connected speech naturally.
- Understand different accents and pronunciation patterns.

---

### Exam Ability

- Predict answers before listening.
- Locate key information quickly.
- Ignore distractors.
- Adapt to different question types.
- Manage time effectively.
- Maintain high accuracy under exam conditions.

---

## Success Criteria

The learner should be able to:

- Score **35–36 correct answers** consistently.
- Complete all four sections confidently.
- Recognize most paraphrases naturally.
- Make very few spelling mistakes.
- Stay calm throughout the entire listening test.

# 2. Test Overview

## Test Structure

| Item | Description |
|------|-------------|
| Sections | 4 |
| Questions | 40 |
| Duration | Approximately 30 minutes |
| Difficulty | Increases from Section 1 → Section 4 |

# 3. Overall Strategy

The following strategies apply to every Listening question type.

---

## 3.1 Language Knowledge

These are the language abilities required for successful listening comprehension.

| Knowledge | Importance | Description |
|------------|------------|-------------|
| Vocabulary | ⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐ | Everyday and academic vocabulary |
| Synonyms | ⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐ | Recognize equivalent expressions |
| Paraphrasing | ⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐ | Understand meaning beyond exact wording |
| Numbers | ⭐⭐⭐⭐ | Phone numbers, prices, quantities, room numbers |
| Dates | ⭐⭐⭐⭐ | Different date formats |
| Letters / Spelling | ⭐⭐⭐ | Correct spelling of names and words |
| Addresses | ⭐⭐⭐ | Street names and locations |
| Connected Speech | ⭐⭐⭐⭐ | Linking, contractions and weak forms |
| Pronunciation | ⭐⭐⭐ | Stress and intonation |
| Signposting Language | ⭐⭐⭐⭐ | firstly, however, finally, in contrast... |

---

## 3.2 Exam Knowledge

These are IELTS-specific strategies rather than language knowledge.

| Strategy | Description |
|-----------|-------------|
| Prediction | Predict missing information before listening |
| Predict Answer Type | Predict noun, number, adjective, verb, etc. |
| Identify Keywords | Find key information before the recording starts |
| Word Limit Awareness | Follow ONE WORD ONLY / TWO WORDS AND A NUMBER requirements |
| Time Management | Use preparation time efficiently |
| Answer Checking | Check spelling, plural forms and grammar |
| Distractor Recognition | Ignore corrected or misleading information |

# 4. Question Type Analysis

Each question type has its own solving strategy and language requirements.

---

## Form Completion

### Purpose

Collect factual information.

### Typical Information

- Name
- Phone Number
- Address
- Date
- Price
- Time
- Room Number

### Solving Strategy

1. Read the form.
2. Predict the answer type.
3. Listen for specific information.
4. Check spelling and grammar.

### Required Language Knowledge

- Numbers
- Letters
- Everyday vocabulary
- Addresses

### Common Mistakes

- Spelling mistakes
- Wrong plural forms
- Ignoring word limits

---

## Note Completion

### Solving Strategy

- Read the notes first.
- Predict the missing word type.
- Follow the speaker's logic.
- Listen for paraphrases.

### Required Language Knowledge

- Vocabulary
- Synonyms
- Academic expressions
- Grammar

---

## Summary Completion

### Solving Strategy

- Read the summary first.
- Predict grammar and part of speech.
- Listen for paraphrases instead of identical words.

### Required Language Knowledge

- Vocabulary
- Synonyms
- Paraphrasing
- Grammar

---

## Table Completion

### Solving Strategy

- Understand the table structure.
- Predict categories.
- Listen for comparisons and numbers.

### Required Language Knowledge

- Numbers
- Categories
- Vocabulary

---

## Multiple Choice

### Solving Strategy

- Read all options first.
- Compare differences between options.
- Focus on meaning instead of keywords.
- Watch for distractors.

### Required Language Knowledge

- Vocabulary
- Synonyms
- Paraphrasing
- Listening comprehension

---

## Matching

### Solving Strategy

- Read all options first.
- Predict possible topics.
- Continue listening even if one answer is missed.

### Required Language Knowledge

- Vocabulary
- Topic understanding
- Synonyms

---

## Map / Plan Labelling

### Solving Strategy

- Identify the starting point.
- Understand direction words.
- Follow the speaker step by step.

### Required Language Knowledge

- Direction vocabulary
- Location expressions
- Spatial relationships

---

## Sentence Completion

### Solving Strategy

- Predict grammar and part of speech.
- Listen for paraphrases.
- Check grammar after completion.

### Required Language Knowledge

- Vocabulary
- Grammar
- Synonyms

---

## Short Answer Questions

### Solving Strategy

- Read the questions first.
- Listen for specific information.
- Follow the word limit carefully.

### Required Language Knowledge

- Vocabulary
- Listening for detail

---
